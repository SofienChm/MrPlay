Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

MrPlay ("we", "our", or "us") is a multi-platform video and content hub for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the MrPlay app ("the App"), how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

No account is required. MrPlay does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personally identifying information to use the App.

  • Advertising data. The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier/IDFA), IP address, device and app information, and usage data in order to deliver and measure ads. This is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Locally stored data. Features such as Favorites, Watch Later, bookmarks, recent activity, and playback progress are stored only on your device using local storage. This data is not transmitted to us or to any of our servers.
  • Web content. The App loads the official mobile websites of third-party platforms (for example YouTube, Twitch, Instagram). Those websites have their own privacy practices and may collect data when you interact with them. Please review each platform's privacy policy directly.

2. Permissions We Use

  • Background audio. The App may continue playing audio while the App is in the background or the screen is locked.
  • Microphone. Microphone access is used only by web content you open (for example voice features on a website). We do not record or store microphone audio.

3. How We Use Information

We use advertising data solely to display ads in the App (via Google AdMob). We do not sell your personal information, and we do not operate our own analytics or data-collection backend.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — serves advertisements. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Third-party websites — the platforms you open inside the App are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

All user-generated data (favorites, bookmarks, watch later, recent activity, playback progress) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to it. Advertising data is processed by Google according to its own policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so it can be removed.

7. Your Choices

  • You can reset your Advertising Identifier or enable "Limit Ad Tracking" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can clear the App's local data by deleting the App from your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@app-miniminds.com
