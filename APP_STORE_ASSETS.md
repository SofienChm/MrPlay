# MrPlay - App Store Assets

## App Name
MrPlay

## Subtitle
Your Video Hub

## Description
MrPlay is a multi-platform video and content hub that brings all your favorite platforms together in one place. With a clean, native iOS experience, MrPlay makes it easy to access YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, Reddit, and 16+ more platforms from a single app.

Features:
• Multi-platform hub with 20+ platforms organized by category
• Persistent mini player that keeps your video playing while you browse
• Background audio support for uninterrupted listening
• Notification center controls for quick play/pause
• Favorites system to save your most-watched content
• Smart search across all platforms
• Dark and light mode support
• Customizable default platform

MrPlay loads the official mobile websites of each platform through a secure WebView, ensuring you always get the latest content and features directly from the source.

## Review Notes
This app uses WKWebView to load the official mobile websites of video and content platforms (youtube.com, twitch.tv, instagram.com, etc.). No content is downloaded, cached, or modified. All video playback occurs through the platforms' own web players. The app provides native iOS features including a multi-platform hub, persistent mini player, and background audio controls that are not available in mobile browsers.
