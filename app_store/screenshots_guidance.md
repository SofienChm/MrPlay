# App Store Screenshots Guidance

## Required Screenshots (6.5" iPhone)

### Screenshot 1: Hub Screen (Primary)
Show the colorful platform grid with gradient background.
- Show the MrPlay title, search bar, and a variety of platform cards
- Ensure multiple platform cards are visible (YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, etc.)
- This is the main screen users see on launch

### Screenshot 2: YouTube Loaded in WebView
Show YouTube (or another platform) loaded in the WebView.
- Show the native browser toolbar (back, forward, title, minimize, close)
- Demonstrate that the app loads the real mobile website
- Do NOT show any ad-blocking effects

### Screenshot 3: Mini Player at Bottom
Show the mini player docked at the bottom of the screen.
- This demonstrates the persistent playback feature
- Show thumbnail, title, play/pause, favorite, and close buttons
- This is a key differentiator from mobile browsers

### Screenshot 4: Settings Screen
Show the settings page.
- Show theme toggle, favorites, clear options
- Demonstrate native iOS-feeling UI

### Screenshot 5 (Optional): Favorites
Show the favorites list with saved videos.
- Demonstrate the Hive-based local storage

## What to AVOID in Screenshots
❌ Do NOT show "Ad-free" or "No ads" messaging anywhere
❌ Do NOT show screenshots that appear to block YouTube ads (if ads appear naturally in the WebView, that's fine)
❌ Do NOT show "Download videos" functionality
❌ Do NOT show any pirated or copyrighted content as the main focus
✅ Emphasis should be on: multi-platform hub, mini player, background audio, native controls
