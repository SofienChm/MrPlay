## Description (1700 char max)
MrPlay brings together your favorite video and content platforms in one beautiful, native iOS experience. Browse, watch, and switch between platforms seamlessly with a mini player that keeps your video playing while you explore.

Key Features:
• Multi-Platform Hub - Access 20+ content platforms from a single beautiful grid
• Persistent Mini Player - Video keeps playing in a compact mini player while you browse other content
• Background Audio - Continue listening to audio even when your screen is off (YouTube, Music, etc.)
• Lock Screen Controls - Play, pause, and control playback from your lock screen and notification center
• Smart Search - Quickly find your favorite platforms and dive into content
• Favorites - Save your favorite videos for quick access later

Supported Platforms:
YouTube, YouTube Music, Twitch, Rumble, Duolingo, Busuu, Babbel, Memrise, Mondly, Instagram, Reddit, Facebook, X (Twitter), Disney+, HBO Max, Prime Video, Pinterest, Quora, 9GAG, iFunny

MrPlay uses WKWebView to load the official mobile websites of these platforms. All video playback occurs through the platforms' own web players. No content is downloaded, cached, or modified.
